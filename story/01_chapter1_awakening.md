# Chapter 1: The Awakening

## Overview
Alkmaeon's first visit to the Temple of Apollo triggers dormant memories. He must explore the ruins, face corrupted spirits, and receive his first divine vision.

---

## Scenes

### Scene 1.1: The Village (Tutorial)
**Location**: Aethon Village

- Player learns basic movement
- Talk to Elder Theron (optional)
- Exit village toward the temple

**Elder Theron Dialogue**:
> "Welcome, young Alkmaeon."
> "The temple on the hill... it calls to you, does it not?"
> "I have seen that look before. Go, but be careful."

---

### Scene 1.2: First Steps in the Ruins
**Location**: Temple of Apollo — Entrance

**Trigger**: Player enters temple for the first time

**Vision Sequence** (auto-advancing):
> *As you step among the shattered pillars, a flash sears through your mind...*
> *Visions of a forgotten past blaze across your thoughts...*
> *Images both alien and unmistakably your own.*

- Player regains control
- Can explore the ruins
- Random encounters with Temple Spirits

---

### Scene 1.3: The Altar
**Location**: Temple of Apollo — Inner Sanctum

**Trigger**: Player interacts with the central altar

**Vision Sequence** (auto-advancing):
> *You touch the cold stone of the altar...*
> *A surge of divine energy courses through you!*
> *You see a great temple, whole and magnificent, filled with worshippers...*
> *A figure in golden robes turns to face you — Apollo himself.*

**Apollo (???)** speaks:
> "Child of Memory... the Shards call to you."
> "Gather them, before the Red Moon rises complete."

> *The vision fades, leaving you breathless but somehow... stronger.*
> *You have learned Memory Flash!*

---

### Scene 1.4: Return to Village
**Location**: Aethon Village

- Elder Theron has new dialogue
- Meet Lyra at the shrine (future party member)
- Story flag set for Chapter 2
