### Boss: Corrupted Remnant of Apollo

**Corrupted Remnant** (HP: 250 — Highest in the game)

**Form**: Apollo's divine avatar, twisted by Typhon's chaos — golden armor cracked, light flickering between radiance and shadow

**Phase 1: Divided Light** (100%-70% HP)
- Switches between Light and Chaos attacks
- Uses: Solar Flare (Light damage to all), Chaos Burst (Shadow damage to one)
- Vulnerability: Exploiting when switching states

**Phase 2: War Within** (70%-40% HP)
- Becomes unstable — attacks randomly target anyone, including itself
- Uses: Fractured Prophecy (random effects), Duality Strike (Light + Shadow combo)
- The real Apollo's voice breaks through occasionally
- **Apollo (struggling):** "Fight... me... I cannot control..."
- Strategy: Heal the party member it targets; it will occasionally hurt itself

**Phase 3: Chaos Dominant** (40%-0% HP)
- Typhon's influence takes full control — pure chaos
- Uses: Typhon's Voice (massive party damage), Reality Shatter (chance to insta-kill — can be blocked with Defend), Consume Light (absorbs Light attacks to heal)
- Weakness: The Shards — combined power breaks through Typhon's control
- Strategy: Alkmaeon must use all three Shards together for ultimate attack

## Design Notes

This chapter is the revelation and emotional climax. Key elements:

1. **The return home**: Emotional grounding before the cosmic climax
2. **The truth**: Alkmaeon as the fourth Shard — not just a chosen one, but a made one
3. **The choice**: Genuine agency — player decides Alkmaeon's fate
4. **The boss**: Corrupted Apollo shows that even gods can fall
5. **The letters and farewells**: Ground the cosmic stakes in human emotion

The choice between transcendence and defiance should feel genuinely difficult. Neither is presented as "correct."
