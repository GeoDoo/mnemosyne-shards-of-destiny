## Scene 5.5: Boss Battle — Echo of Typhon

**Boss: Echo of Typhon** (HP: 220)

**Form**: A partially-formed Typhon — dragon heads, storm-body, partially real

**Phase 1: Storm Form** (100%-60% HP)
- Highly mobile, hard to hit
- Uses: Thunder Roar (party-wide lightning), Chaos Claw (high single damage), Storm Surge (random targeting)
- Strategy: Consistent damage, keep healed

**Phase 2: Dragon Heads** (60%-30% HP)
- Grows additional dragon heads that act as sub-targets
- Main body takes reduced damage while heads live
- Dragon Head A: Breathes fire
- Dragon Head B: Breathes ice
- Dragon Head C: Breathes poison
- Strategy: Kill heads to expose main body

**Phase 3: Desperate Chaos** (30%-0% HP)
- Heads regenerate, body becomes unstable
- Uses: Primordial Scream (massive damage, charges for 2 turns), Chaos Unbound (random effects on all combatants)
- Vulnerability: The Shards — using them together amplifies damage massively
- Strategy: Alkmaeon must use both Shard powers in sequence for "Memory Burst" attack

**Battle Dialogue:**

**Turn 1:**
> **Echo of Typhon:** "YOUR ORDER CRUMBLES BEFORE TRUE POWER!"
> **Alkmaeon:** "Then let's see how true power handles light and silence!"

**Phase 1 Transition:**

> "PERHAPS I UNDERESTIMATED YOU. NO MORE."

**Phase 2 Transition:**
> **Echo of Typhon:** "YOU FIGHT WELL. BUT CAN YOU ENDURE?"

## Design Notes

This chapter is the climax of Act III and must accomplish:

1. **Typhon as a character**: Not a monster, but a force with a perspective
2. **Alkmaeon's crisis**: Questioning his identity, purpose, and autonomy
3. **All three Shards together**: The power increases, but so does the burden
