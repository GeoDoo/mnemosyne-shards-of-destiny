# Prologue: The Chorus of Ruin

> *The opening sequence that establishes the mythic backstory and introduces the player to Thespiae.*

---

## Scene 0.1: The Cosmic Prologue

**Type**: Cinematic (auto-advancing)  
**Visuals**: Dark screen, ancient Greek art style illustrations fade in/out  
**Audio**: Low, ominous choir; distant thunder

---

### Narration Sequence

*[Black screen — text fades in one line at a time]*

> Before the age of men...

> Before the memory of mortals...

> The world trembled.

*[Illustration: Typhon rising — serpentine coils, storm and lightning]*

> Typhon, born of Gaia's fury, rose against the heavens.

> A hundred dragon heads. A voice like thunder.

> The father of monsters.

*[Illustration: Zeus hurling lightning]*

> Lightning clashed with storm.

> The heavens burned.

> And Zeus, king of gods, cast him down...

*[Illustration: Typhon falling into darkness beneath mountains]*

> ...burying him beneath the roots of the earth.

> Beneath the weight of mountains.

> Beneath the silence of ages.

*[Beat — darkness]*

> Yet chaos does not die.

> It only sleeps.

*[Illustration: Three divine figures — Mnemosyne, Hecate, Apollo]*

> To guard against his return, the gods wove the Order of Destiny.

> A harmony of fate, law, and remembrance.

*[Illustration: Mnemosyne at a pool of still water]*

> At its heart stood Mnemosyne...

> Titaness of memory.

> Keeper of all that was, and all that must not be forgotten.

*[Illustration: Hecate at a crossroads, torch in hand]*

> At its borders walked Hécate...

> Torch in hand.

> Guiding souls across the thresholds between life, death, and truth.

*[Illustration: Apollo's temple, radiant with light]*

> And Apollo, god of light and prophecy...

> Bound the order with his sacred temples.

> Places where mortals might glimpse the shape of fate.

*[Beat — illustrations fade]*

> For countless ages, the order held.

> The buried one slept.

> And the world forgot the war that nearly ended it.

*[Illustration: A crystal shattering into fragments]*

> Yet nothing eternal remains unbroken.

> Somewhere — in the forgotten corners of the world...

> A fragment of this order was lost.

> A Shard of Destiny.

*[Illustration: Ruined temple on a mountainside]*

> Hidden in a sanctuary of Apollo...

> Crumbling.

> Waiting.

*[Final beat — text alone on black]*

> Waiting to be remembered.

*[Fade to white]*

---

## Scene 0.2: The Village Awakens

**Type**: Gameplay introduction  
**Location**: Thespiae — Alkmaeon's home  
**Time**: Early morning  
**Audio**: Birdsong, distant sheep bells, peaceful village ambience

---

### Opening Narration

*[Fade from white to interior of a small stone house]*

> In a quiet mountain village called Thespiae...

> A boy grows up among shepherds and farmers.

> Raised in the shadow of ancient legends he barely understands.

*[Camera pans to show ALKMAEON waking up, sitting on a simple bed]*

> His name is Alkmaeon.

> "Mighty in wrath" — though he knows not why his mother chose it.

> Today feels different. The air hums with something he cannot name.

*[ALKMAEON's MOTHER enters]*

---

### Dialogue: Mother

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "Alkmaeon, you're finally awake."

> "I've let you sleep long enough. The others have been up since dawn."

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "Your breakfast is on the table. Eat, then go see what Epimenides needs."

> "He asked for you specifically this morning."

*[She pauses at the doorway]*

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "And stay away from that old temple on the hill."

> "I don't like the way you look at it."

*[She exits]*

**Location**: Thespiae — Exterior  
**Time**: Morning  
**Objectives**: 
- Learn movement controls

> Stone houses cluster around a central square. A well. A small shrine to the gods.

> To the east, Epimenides's house stands apart from the others.

## Scene 0.4: Epimenides

**Type**: Story dialogue  
**Location**: Epimenides's house  
**Trigger**: Player enters Epimenides's home

---

### Interior: Elder's House

> *The Elder's home smells of incense and old scrolls.*
> *Shelves line the walls, filled with texts you cannot read.*
> *Maps of places you've never heard of.*

*[ELDER THERON sits by the fire, waiting]*

---

### Dialogue: Epimenides (First Meeting)

**Epimenides:**
> "Alkmaeon. Come in, come in."

> "Sit. We have much to discuss."

*[Alkmaeon sits]*

**Epimenides:**
> "You've been dreaming again. I can see it in your eyes."

> "Dreams of fire. Of light. Of things you cannot name."

**Epimenides:**
> "Do not look so surprised. I was young once too."

> "And I have studied the old ways long enough to recognize the signs."

---

**Epimenides:**
> "Tell me, boy. What do you know of the temple on the mountain?"

*[Player choice]*

**Option A: "Mother says to stay away from it."**
> **Epimenides:** "Your mother is wise to be cautious."
> "But caution and ignorance are not the same thing."

**Option B: "I dream about it sometimes."**
> **Epimenides:** "Yes. I thought as much."
> "The temple calls to those who are meant to hear."

**Option C: "It's just old ruins."**
> **Epimenides:** "Just ruins? No, boy."
> "That temple was sacred to Apollo himself. A place where mortals could glimpse fate."
> "Its stones remember what we have forgotten."

---

**Epimenides:**
> "Listen carefully, Alkmaeon."

> "Long ago, the gods waged a war against chaos itself."

> "They won — but victory came at a cost."

> "Fragments of their power were scattered. Hidden. Lost."

**Epimenides:**
> "The temple on our mountain was one of Apollo's sanctuaries."

> "It fell to ruin centuries ago. But the power within it..."

> "...that power never truly faded."

---

**Epimenides:**
> "Tonight, a Red Moon will rise."

> "It is an omen. The heavens aligning in ways they have not for generations."

> "I believe the temple will... react."

**Epimenides:**
> "I am too old to climb that mountain."

> "But you, Alkmaeon — you hear its call."

> "Go. See what awaits you there."

> "But go with open eyes. And return before nightfall."

---

*[Epimenides gives Alkmaeon a small item]*

**Epimenides:**
> "Take this. An old charm of protection."

> "It won't save you from your fate. But it may buy you time to understand it."

> ***Received: Charm of Epimenides***
> *A small bronze disc inscribed with ancient symbols.*
> *Effect: +5 Defense*

---

**Epimenides:**
> "Go now. The mountain awaits."

> "And Alkmaeon..."

> "Trust what you see. Even if it seems impossible."

*[Story flag set: `theron_quest_given`]*

## Scene 0.7: The First Vision

**Type**: Vision sequence (auto-advancing)  
**Visuals**: Fragmented, dreamlike imagery  
**Audio**: Distorted choir, crackling energy, heartbeat

---

### Vision Sequence

*[Rapid flashes — player sees fragments]*

> A burning sky.

> A war between light and storm.

*[Flash: Apollo raising his hand against a shadowy mass]*

> A crystal — whole and radiant — pulsing with divine light.

*[Flash: The crystal shatters]*

> It shatters.

> Fragments scatter like falling stars.

*[Flash: A veiled woman in darkness, holding a torch]*

> A figure in the darkness.

> A veiled woman. Twin torches.

> Hécate.

> She turns toward you.

*[The vision freezes — Hecate's eyes meet yours]*

> Her eyes meet yours across the abyss of time.

> She does not speak.

> But you feel her presence — ancient, patient, watching.

*[Vision ends — screen whites out]*

---

### After the Vision

*[Player wakes on the temple floor]*

> You gasp awake.

> Stone beneath your cheek. Dust in your lungs.

> How long were you unconscious?

> The sun has moved. Shadows have shifted.

> The temple is silent — but you are changed.

> Something has awakened inside you.

> A flicker of light behind your eyes. A warmth in your chest.

> You don't understand it yet.

> But you know — with absolute certainty — that nothing will ever be the same.

*[Player regains control]*

*[Story flag set: `first_vision_complete`]*

## Scene 0.9: The Red Moon Rises

**Type**: Cinematic / Story event  
**Location**: Thespiae — Evening  
**Trigger**: Player returns to village after first temple visit  
**Audio**: Ominous drone, villagers murmuring

---

### Village at Dusk

*[The village has changed — NPCs gathered in the square, looking at the sky]*

> Something is wrong.

> The villagers stand in clusters, faces upturned.

> Whispers ripple through the crowd.

*[Player looks up — cutscene]*

---

### The Red Moon

> The moon rises over the mountain.

> But it is not silver.

> It is red. Deep, bloody red.

> The color of old wounds. Of prophecy. Of endings.

---

*[ELDER THERON approaches]*

**Epimenides:**
> "Alkmaeon. You've returned."

> "I see the temple has shown you... something."

*[He looks at the moon]*

**Epimenides:**
> "The Red Moon. It has not risen in living memory."

> "My grandmother spoke of it. Her grandmother before her."

**Epimenides:**
> "When the Red Moon bleeds, the earth remembers..."

> "...and the buried one stirs."

---

**Epimenides:**
> "What did you see, boy? In the temple?"

*[Player choice]*

**Option A: "I saw a war. Gods and monsters."**
> **Epimenides:** "The Titanomachy. The war that shaped the world."
> "If the temple showed you that... then you are more important than I feared."

**Option B: "I saw a woman with torches. Watching me."**
> **Epimenides:** "Hécate. The goddess of crossroads."
> "If she has taken notice of you... your path is no longer your own."

**Option C: "I don't know. It was too fast."**
> **Epimenides:** "The visions will become clearer. For better or worse."

---

**Epimenides:**
> "Listen to me carefully, Alkmaeon."

> "What you experienced was not a dream. It was a memory."

> "Divine memory. Cosmic truth, pressed into your mind."

**Epimenides:**
> "The temple chose you. Or perhaps... you were always meant to be chosen."

> "Either way, your life in this village is ending."

> "A greater fate calls you."

---

*[Alkmaeon's MOTHER pushes through the crowd]*

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "Alkmaeon! Thank the gods you're safe!"

> "I told you to stay away from that place!"

*[She sees the Red Moon, pales]*

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "No... not this. Not my son."

**Epimenides:**
> "It is already done, Thalia. The boy has been marked."

**Alkmaeon's Mother:**
> "Then unmarked him! You're the Elder — do something!"

**Epimenides:**
> "Some things cannot be undone."

> "Only faced."

---

### Night Falls

*[The crowd disperses. Alkmaeon's mother leads him home, silent.]*

> That night, you do not sleep.

> The Red Moon hangs outside your window.

> The visions return — stronger, clearer.

> You see the temple whole. You see Apollo. You see Hécate at the crossroads.

> And beneath it all, you feel something stirring.

> Something vast. Something buried.

> Something waiting to wake.

*[Screen fades to black]*

---

> You know what you must do.

> At dawn, you will leave.

*[Story flag set: `red_moon_witnessed`]*

*[End of Prologue]*
