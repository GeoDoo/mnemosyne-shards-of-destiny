### Boss: Typhon (Transcendence Path)

**Typhon** (HP: 300 — God-tier)

**Form**: Titanic — the party is ants before a giant. Alkmaeon (transformed) is the only one who can truly damage him.

**Mechanic**: Theano and Brasidas support Alkmaeon by maintaining barriers and healing. Alkmaeon channels the Order's power.

**Phase 1: The Storm Rises** (100%-70% HP)
- Typhon attacks with catastrophic area damage
- Party must survive while Alkmaeon charges
- Uses: World Shaker (damage all), Hundred Heads (multi-strike), Primordial Roar (stun chance)
- Alkmaeon's transformed state lets him charge "Order's Wrath" — massive damage after 3 turns of channeling

**Phase 2: Clash of Eternities** (70%-30% HP)
- Direct combat between Alkmaeon (Order) and Typhon (Chaos)
- Party supports with buffs and heals
- Uses: Chaos Incarnate (random devastation), Consume Reality (instant-kill attack — can be blocked by party using Defend together)
- Alkmaeon can use: Memory's Light (heavy Light damage), Silence's Peace (party heal), Ruin's Truth (break Typhon's defense for one turn)

**Phase 3: Final Defiance** (30%-0% HP)
- Typhon grows desperate — attacks become faster, more chaotic
- Uses: Deathless Fury (constant damage to all), Final Chaos (massive charge-up attack that ends the world if not stopped)
- Alkmaeon must use: Order's Binding — the ultimate skill that seals Typhon forever

### Boss: Typhon (Defiance Path)

**Typhon** (HP: 300 — same tier, but fought differently)

**Form**: Same titanic form, but the party must work together as equals

**Mechanic**: No transformation — must use teamwork, strategy, and the Shards' powers individually rather than merged

**Phase 1: Against the Storm** (100%-60% HP)
- Full party combat — all three contribute
- Alkmaeon wields the Shards separately, switching between them
- Uses Shard of Light for damage, Silence for healing, Ruin for debuffs

- Combo attacks unlock — party members can chain abilities

- Brasidas + Alkmaeon: Shadow Memory (critical strike + defense down)
- All Three: Trinity Strike (heavy damage, breaks Typhon's phase)
- Typhon grows more erratic — Hundred Heads, Reality Tear

**Phase 3: Mortal Defiance** (30%-0% HP)
- Typhon targets Alkmaeon specifically — recognizes him as the threat
- Uses: Focus Destruction (massive single-target), Final Chaos (same charge attack)
- Final move: Alkmaeon must use all three Shards simultaneously — NOT merging, but harmonizing
- ***Memory's Chains*** — binds Typhon through the power of mortal will

## Thematic Resolution

- **Memory vs. Oblivion**: Memory triumphs through preservation
- **Order vs. Chaos**: Order maintained, but chaos endures in potential
- **Mortality vs. Divinity**: Both paths are valid — divinity or humanity
- **Fate vs. Choice**: Choice defines us, regardless of outcome

---

## Design Notes

The dual endings honor player agency while maintaining narrative coherence:

1. **Transcendence**: The classical hero's sacrifice — bittersweet but noble
2. **Defiance**: The modern hero's rebellion — triumphant but still burdened

Neither ending is "better" — they reflect different philosophies:
- Transcendence: Some things are worth more than one life
- Defiance: Every life has inherent value that cannot be sacrificed

The post-credits scene sets up potential sequel hooks while honoring the closure of this story.
