# Master Timeline

> **Note**: Events marked with ⚡ are flashbacks/visions. Events marked with 🔴 occur during the Red Moon.

---

## Primordial Era (Before Time)

- **The Titanomachy** — Zeus defeats Typhon, buries him beneath the earth
- **Order of Destiny** woven by the gods — Mnemosyne at its heart
- **Hecate** appointed guardian of thresholds between worlds
- A **Shard of Destiny** is hidden in Apollo's sanctuary as failsafe

---

## Ancient Past (~500 years before story)

- Temple of Apollo built on the mountain above Thespiae
- Well of Mnemosyne consecrated nearby (Orphic rites)
- Priests maintain the sacred sites

---

## Recent Past (~50 years before story)

- Temple of Apollo falls into ruin (cause unknown)
- Villagers avoid the mountain — superstition grows
- Old prophecies about the "Red Moon" become legends

---

## Alkmaeon's Birth (~16 years before story)

- Boy born in Thespiae under unusual celestial signs
- Mother dies in childbirth (or shortly after)
- Raised by village, no knowledge of his origin
- **Hidden truth**: He is a vessel created by Mnemosyne

---

## Present Day — Game Events

### Chapter 1: The Awakening
- Alkmaeon drawn to the ruined Temple of Apollo
- ⚡ First vision: burning skies, war between light and storm
- ⚡ Sees Hecate watching him
- 🔴 **Red Moon rises** that night
- Village elders speak the prophecy

### Chapter 2: The Call of Memory
- Alkmaeon finds the Well of Mnemosyne
- ⚡ Visions reveal the Shards and Typhon's weakening prison
- Hecate appears, points him east
- Meets **Theano** (priestess sent to investigate disturbance)
- Journey begins

### Chapter 3: Memory of Light
- Travel to the first shrine (location TBD)
- ⚡ Relives Apollo's victory over darkness
- First Shard retrieved
- Meets **Brasidas** on Delos

### Chapter 4: Memory of Silence
- Second shrine in a place of death/forgetting
- ⚡ Sees how gods forget mortals
- Second Shard retrieved
- Alkmaeon's identity begins to blur

### Chapter 5: Memory of Ruin
- Third shrine at the edge of Typhon's influence
- ⚡ Witnesses Typhon's rebellion — not mindless, but ancient
- Third Shard retrieved
- 🔴 Red Moon grows closer/larger

### Chapter 6: The Revelation
- Reach Typhon's Sanctum at Red Moon's peak
- **Truth revealed**: The final Shard is within Alkmaeon himself
- He is Mnemosyne's living memory
- Hecate presents the final choice

### Chapter 7: The Binding
- Return to Temple of Apollo
- All Shards gathered
- Alkmaeon channels Mnemosyne's power
- Typhon bound again
- Dawn breaks, Red Moon fades
- Alkmaeon transformed — half mortal, half divine
