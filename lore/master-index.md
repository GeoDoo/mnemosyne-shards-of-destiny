# Mnemosyne: Shards of Destiny — Master Index

> **Purpose**: High-level reference for AI and writers. Read this FIRST before drafting any narrative content.

## Core Premise

A nameless boy from Thespiae discovers he is a living vessel of **Mnemosyne's memory**, destined to recover the scattered **Shards of Destiny** before **Typhon** breaks free from his ancient prison.

## Three Divine Forces

| Deity | Domain | Role in Story | Symbol |
|-------|--------|---------------|--------|
| **Mnemosyne** | Memory, cosmic order | Source of the Shards; the boy's origin | The Well, The Mirror |
| **Hecate** | Crossroads, thresholds | Guide and watcher; appears at turning points | The Torch, The Crossroads |
| **Apollo** | Light, prophecy, harmony | His ruined temple is where it begins and ends | The Lyre, The Temple |

## The Antagonist

**Typhon** — Primordial chaos buried by Zeus. Not evil, but entropy itself. His return is nature reasserting itself against order.

## Themes

- **Memory vs. Oblivion** — What is lost when we forget?
- **Order vs. Chaos** — Can harmony be restored?
- **Mortality vs. Divinity** — What does it cost to become more than human?
- **Fate vs. Choice** — Do we choose destiny, or does it choose us?

## Tone

**Greek tragedy** meets **JRPG**. Chorus-style narration. Fatalistic but not nihilistic — sacrifice has meaning.
