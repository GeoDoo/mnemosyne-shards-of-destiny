## Key Locations

### Thespiae Village
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Boeotia, central Greece |
| **Chapter** | 1 (start), returns possible |
| **Tone** | Pastoral, quiet, superstitious |

**Description**: A small mountain village of shepherds and farmers. Ancient temples loom above, but the villagers avoid them. Life is simple; the old stories are just stories — until the Red Moon rises.

**Key Areas**:
- Village square (market, well)
- Alkmaeon's home
- Elder Epimenides' house
- Path to the mountain

---

### Temple of Apollo (Ruined)
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Mountain above Thespiae |
| **Chapter** | 1 (first visit), 7 (final) |
| **Tone** | Sacred, melancholic, powerful |

**Description**: Broken columns and fallen statues. An altar cracked by time. Once a place of pilgrimage; now abandoned. The air hums with residual divine energy.

**Key Areas**:
- Entrance colonnade
- Main sanctuary (altar)
- Hidden inner sanctum (Chapter 7)

---

### Well of Mnemosyne
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Hidden spring near the temple |
| **Chapter** | 2 |
| **Tone** | Mystical, quiet, introspective |

**Description**: A sacred spring whispered of in Orphic rites. The water is unnaturally still and reflective. Drinking from it brings visions — or madness.

---

### Delos (Sacred Island)
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Aegean Sea |
| **Chapter** | 3 (Memory of Light) |
| **Tone** | Sun-bleached, holy, ancient |

**Description**: Birthplace of Apollo and Artemis. The island is sacred — no one may be born or die here. Perfect location for the Memory of Light shrine.

**Key Areas**:
- Harbor
- Terrace of the Lions
- Shrine of Light (dungeon)

---

### Levadeia (Oracle of Trophonius)
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Boeotia |
| **Chapter** | 4 (Memory of Silence) |
| **Tone** | Eerie, silent, subterranean |

**Description**: Site of an ancient oracle where seekers descended into a cave and emerged changed — often unable to laugh again. A place where memory meets forgetting.

**Key Areas**:
- Surface town
- Cave descent
- Shrine of Silence (dungeon)

---

### Nekromanteion (Oracle of the Dead)
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Epirus, northwest |
| **Chapter** | 5 (Memory of Ruin) |
| **Tone** | Dark, liminal, haunted |

**Description**: The ancient Greeks believed this was a gate to the underworld. Pilgrims came to speak with the dead. The boundary between worlds is thin here.

**Key Areas**:
- Surface ruins
- Labyrinthine descent
- Shrine of Ruin (dungeon)

---

### Typhon's Sanctum
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Region** | Deep beneath the earth |
| **Chapter** | 6 (The Revelation) |
| **Tone** | Oppressive, primordial, terrifying |

**Description**: Not a place mortals should ever reach. The earth groans. The air is hot and sulphurous. Typhon's heartbeat echoes in the walls.

**Key Areas**:
- Descent through volcanic tunnels
- The Heart Chamber (final confrontation)

---

## Travel Notes

- **Overworld travel**: Represented by map transitions, not open world
- **Sea travel**: Required to reach Delos
- **Each location** should feel distinct in color palette and music
- **Random encounters**: Themed to region (spirits near Nekromanteion, etc.)
