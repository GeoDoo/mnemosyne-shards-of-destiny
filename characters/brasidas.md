## Background

A celebrated Spartan general who led victorious campaigns across Greece. While he was away at war, his wife **Arete** fell ill and died. He never forgave himself for not being there.

Since then, Brasidas has wandered — no longer fighting for Sparta, but unable to stop fighting. He seeks an honorable death that never comes.

**Meeting the Party**: Encountered on Delos, where he's investigating strange phenomena (or perhaps seeking a worthy end). Alkmaeon's mission gives him purpose again.
