## Appearance

- **Build**: Lean, wiry — a shepherd's frame
- **Hair**: Dark brown, slightly unkempt
- **Eyes**: Cyan/teal — unnaturally bright (hint of divine origin)
- **Clothing**: Simple Greek chiton, later gains a traveler's cloak
- **Distinguishing**: Eyes glow faintly during visions

---

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Curious** — Drawn to mysteries others avoid
- **Reluctant** — Does not seek glory; duty is thrust upon him
- **Empathetic** — Feels the weight of memories he absorbs
- **Conflicted** — Struggles with his fading humanity

### Flaws
- **Self-doubt** — Questions if he is worthy
- **Isolation** — The visions set him apart
- **Rage potential** — His name's meaning foreshadows darkness

### Arc
1. **Act I**: Ordinary boy, confused by visions
2. **Act II-III**: Reluctant hero, growing into role
3. **Act IV**: Identity crisis — is he still himself?
4. **Act V**: Acceptance — chooses to be more than human

---

## Background

Born in Thespiae under unusual celestial signs. Mother died in childbirth. Raised by the village with no knowledge of his true origin.

**The Hidden Truth**: Alkmaeon is not a normal human. He is a vessel created by Mnemosyne — a living container for cosmic memory, born to guard against Typhon's return.
