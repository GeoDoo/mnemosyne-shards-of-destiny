## Appearance

- **Build**: Thin, stooped with age
- **Hair**: White, balding, with wisps on sides
- **Eyes**: Pale, but sharp — misses nothing
- **Clothing**: Off-white Greek himation with purple trim
- **Distinguishing**: Laurel wreath (hint of past status); long white beard

---

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Wise** — Knows more than he says
- **Cryptic** — Speaks in riddles and old sayings
- **Protective** — Cares for the village, especially Alkmaeon
- **Fearful** — Knows enough to be afraid

### Arc (NPC)
- Appears primarily in Chapter 1
- May reappear in Chapter 7 (bookend)
- Represents the ordinary world Alkmaeon leaves behind

---

## Background

The oldest man in Thespiae. He remembers when the Temple of Apollo was still visited by pilgrims. He knows the old prophecies — and fears what the Red Moon means.

**Hidden Knowledge**: Epimenides may know more about Alkmaeon's origin than he reveals. He recognized something unusual about the boy from birth.

---

## Role in Story

### Chapter 1
- First to notice Alkmaeon's strange behavior
- Recites the prophecy about the Red Moon
- Warns Alkmaeon not to go to the temple (but knows he must)
- Gives him his blessing when he leaves

### Chapter 7 (Optional)
- If the party returns to Thespiae, he can provide emotional closure
- Sees what Alkmaeon has become
- Final words of wisdom or farewell

## Dialogue Notes

- Speaks slowly, deliberately
- Uses old proverbs and myths
- Never gives straight answers to cosmic questions
- Voice of the ordinary world's wisdom and limits

### Sample Lines
- *"The mountain remembers what the valley forgets."*
- *"When the Red Moon bleeds, the earth remembers — and the buried one stirs."*
- *"I always knew you were different, boy. I prayed I was wrong."*
- *"Go, then. The gods have chosen. Who am I to argue with fate?"*
