## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Devout** — Her faith is genuine, not performative
- **Compassionate** — Heals because she feels others' pain
- **Curious** — Seeks knowledge of the mysteries
- **Brave** — Quiet courage, not flashy heroism

### Flaws
- **Naivety** — Sheltered upbringing at the sanctuary
- **Rigid** — Sometimes struggles with moral grey areas
- **Self-sacrifice** — Puts others before herself to a fault

### Arc
1. **Introduction**: Sent by oracles to investigate; meets Alkmaeon
2. **Growth**: Learns the world is more complex than temple teachings
3. **Crisis**: Witnesses things that challenge her faith
4. **Resolution**: Faith evolves — gods are real but not what she thought

---

## Background

Raised at the Sanctuary of Demeter in Eleusis, trained in the Eleusinian Mysteries. When the oracles sensed a cosmic disturbance, she was sent to investigate — the youngest priestess ever given such a mission.

**Hidden Depth**: Theano's healing gifts are unusually strong. She may have divine ancestry (Demeter's lineage), though she doesn't know it.
